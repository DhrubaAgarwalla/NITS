Governance & Mission

About NITS-FIIE

Building a robust entrepreneurial bedrock at NIT Silchar. Harnessing high-calibre academic intellect, advanced engineering resources, and a state-supported incubation infrastructure.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a world-class technology business incubator in Northeast India, acting as a pivotal force for regional economic development through scalable high-impact technology startup creations.

Our Mission

To nurture scientific minds, promote intellectual property commercialization, provide high-quality mentoring, seed capital access, and offer cutting-edge engineering testing spaces.

Core Values

Commitment to innovation transparency, robust academic rigor, regional inclusivity, absolute integrity, and a supportive culture for bold entrepreneurial risk-taking.
