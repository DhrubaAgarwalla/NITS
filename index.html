Who We Are

Driving the Innovation Ecosystem at NIT Silchar

The **NITS-Foundation for Innovation, Incubation and Entrepreneurship** (NITS-FIIE) is a non-profit section 8 company established at the **National Institute of Technology Silchar**.

Mirroring top academic incubators nationwide, NITS-FIIE serves as the central node for intellectual property commercialization, contract R&D partnerships, and early-stage startup nurturing. We enable faculty, researchers, and regional entrepreneurs to translate cutting-edge scientific achievements into viable industrial entities.
