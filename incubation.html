Nurturing Ventures

Incubation Programs

Empowering technology entrepreneurs with physical workspaces, high-end design tools, legal advisory pipelines, seed investments, and access to active venture capitalist networks.

How We Support

Startup Support Schemes

Pre-Incubation

Duration: 6 Months

Ideal for students and academic researchers with raw ideas. Provides structured validation, market opportunity assessments, proof-of-concept testing resources, and initial mockup spaces.

  • Idea Validation Bootcamps
  • Mockup & Design support
  • Shared hot-desk spaces

Physical Incubation

Duration: 18 - 24 Months

For incorporated startups seeking dedicated office suites. Includes private workspaces, high-speed fiber internet, and access to institutional engineering fabrication labs.

  • Private office workspace
  • Supercomputing lab access
  • Patent filing assistance

Virtual Incubation

Duration: Flexible

Designed for remote founders. Gain continuous mentorship, access to corporate partners, legal advice, and remote seed capital opportunities without relocating to Silchar.

  • Bi-weekly virtual advisory
  • Pitch prep reviews
  • Corporate vendor discounts
Roadmap

Application & Growth Process

Step 01

Submit Online Pitch

Complete our online application form with your team info, patent descriptions, business model, and raw technology prototype files.

Step 02

Screening & Interview

Our advisory panel evaluates applications. Selected startups present to the FIIE Advisory Committee for final selection.

Step 03

Onboarding & Space Allocation

Startups complete legal agreements, finalize seed capital terms, set up office suites, and start lab onboarding sessions.

Step 04

Scale & Graduate

Benefit from monthly review panels, strategic corporate collaborations, funding events, and graduate within 24 months.
